  • Heuristic evaluation: reviewed the original interface solo against Nielsen's 10 usability heuristics, rating each issue's severity (0–4).
  • Redesign: rebuilt the interface end-to-end based on those findings (see figure above).
  • Usability study: moderated, in-person sessions with 6 participants of mixed technical background, think-aloud protocol, followed by the SUS questionnaire.
  • Architecture: built behind a swappable data layer, so the live backend connects without a rewrite.
